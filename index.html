HISTORICAL FIELD NOTE / 01

Chapters in volcanic history.

Mayon is the most active volcano in the Philippines. Review four defining moments in its recorded history.

CHAPTER 01 · FEB 1, 1814

The Great Eruption

Mayon's most destructive recorded eruption buried the town of Cagsawa under ash and volcanic mudflows, leaving only its stone church belfry standing.

CHAPTER 02 · JUNE 1897

Seven Days of Fire

A violent 7-day Plinian eruption sent glowing pyroclastic flows down the eastern slopes, reshaping villages along the Albay coastline.

CHAPTER 03 · 1993 – 2000

Modern Pyroclastic Flows

Sudden phreatic explosions and incandescent lava fountains prompted major evacuations and advanced PHIVOLCS volcano monitoring systems.

CHAPTER 04 · 2018 – 2023

Lava Fountains & Domes

Spectacular Strombolian lava fountaining and slow summit dome growth illuminated the night sky over Legazpi City and Daraga.

CULTURAL MONOGRAPH

The Legend of Daragang Magayon.

In Bicolano oral tradition, "Magayon" means beautiful. The volcano is said to have risen from the grave of the maiden Daragang Magayon and her beloved warrior Panganoron.
